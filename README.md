# So You Want To Be A Ham
This repo is my (KD3BTQ's) collection of everything you might want to get started in HAM radio. There are further [resources](#Resources) that are also in this repo and referenced throughout the document below. 


## What Peaked Your Interest in HAM? 
The first question to ask yourself is "what peaked your interest in amateur radio?". This will help you launch into the community with a grounding point, something you can come back to and help focus your time, money, and energy into. You'll find everyone in the community has their own priorities, and while advice and experience should always be welcome, your decision matrix will be different from everyone else because what interests *you* and keeps *you* coming back to HAM radio is different than everyone else. 

Not really sure how to "categorize" your interest? Here are a few reason you may find others tinkering with radios on the weekend: 

- You're interested in global communications that don't rely on someone else's network (Starlink, Satphones, internet, etc)
- You're interested in the physics of antennas and radio propagation
- You think restoring old-school equipment is cool and want to preserve the art of "the ways of doing things"
- You want to learn more about circuits, batteries, and electrical engineering
- You want to do satellite communication stuff 
- You're interested more in learning about radio for emergencies or "grid-down scenarios"
